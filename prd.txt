ECOSPHERE REWARDS
Product Requirements Document (PRD)
An intelligent, gamified sustainability platform using Python, Flask, MySQL and modern web technologies
Version: 1.0 | Project Type: Academic / Research Project | Status: Proposed
1. Executive Summary
EcoSphere Rewards is a data-driven sustainability platform that encourages users to perform, verify and consistently maintain environmentally responsible activities. The platform converts real-world actions such as recycling, cycling, public transport usage, tree planting, energy saving and plastic reduction into measurable environmental impact, EcoPoints, achievements and redeemable rewards.
The enhanced version introduces activity verification, an Eco Wallet with immutable-style transaction history, personalized challenges, impact forecasting, anomaly detection, community competitions, QR-based event verification, reward inventory management and analytics. Python handles business logic and recommendation services, while MySQL provides normalized relational storage and transaction management.
2. Problem Statement
Existing sustainability initiatives often depend on awareness alone. Users may not have a simple way to measure the environmental effect of their daily actions, remain motivated over time, or receive meaningful feedback. Organizations also lack a centralized system for tracking participation and verifying sustainability activities.
3. Product Vision
Build a trustworthy sustainability ecosystem where every verified eco-friendly action becomes measurable impact, every measurable impact creates motivation, and aggregated participation helps communities understand their environmental contribution.
4. Objectives
Track diverse eco-friendly activities with configurable measurement units.
Calculate points and environmental impact using database-driven conversion factors.
Verify selected activities through QR codes, evidence uploads or organizer approval.
Use streaks, levels, badges, challenges and leaderboards to encourage consistency.
Provide personalized challenges and recommendations from historical behavior.
Maintain a complete Eco Wallet transaction history for points earned and spent.
Enable reward redemption with inventory, eligibility and transaction controls.
Provide user, organization and administrator analytics.
Detect unusual activity patterns and route suspicious submissions for review.

9. Innovative Features
Eco Impact Passport — A lifetime profile showing verified CO₂e reduction, waste diverted, water/energy savings and milestones.
Eco Score — A 0–100 sustainability score combining consistency, diversity of actions, verified impact and recent activity.
Impact Forecast — Estimate a user's potential monthly impact if current sustainable behavior continues.
Smart Challenge Generator — Python service selects challenges based on activity history, goals, streaks and difficulty.
Trust Score — Verification reliability score based on approved/rejected submissions and verification method.
QR Eco Events — Organizations create events with expiring QR tokens to verify attendance or participation.
Community Missions — Groups work toward a shared target such as collectively diverting 100 kg of waste.
Eco Wallet — Every point movement is recorded as a transaction rather than simply overwriting a balance.
Anomaly Detection — Rules identify implausible quantities, rapid repeated submissions and abnormal patterns.
Reward Tiers — Rewards can be unlocked by both points and eligibility conditions such as level or badge.

11. Important SQL Capabilities
JOIN users, activities, activity_types and impact_records for dashboards.
GROUP BY activity/date/month for environmental analytics.
Window functions for ranking users without duplicating leaderboard logic.
Transactions for reward redemption: validate balance → lock/check stock → debit wallet → reduce stock → create redemption.
Views for common reports such as monthly impact and active-user statistics.
Indexes on user_id, activity_date, status, challenge_id and transaction timestamps.
Constraints and foreign keys to protect referential integrity.
Stored procedures may be used selectively for controlled administrative/reporting operations.
12. Python / Flask Architecture
Recommended architecture: Flask application + service layer + repository/data-access layer. Keep route handlers thin and place calculations, wallet operations, challenge evaluation and verification rules in services.
app.py / config.py — application setup and configuration
routes/auth.py — authentication endpoints
routes/activity.py — activity APIs
routes/challenge.py — challenge APIs
routes/reward.py — reward and redemption APIs
routes/admin.py — administration APIs
services/impact_service.py — environmental calculations
services/wallet_service.py — atomic points operations
services/gamification_service.py — XP, streaks and badges
services/recommendation_service.py — personalized suggestions
services/verification_service.py — QR/evidence/review workflow
repositories/ — database access functions
templates/ — server-rendered pages
static/ — CSS, JS, images and charts

16. Security & Data Integrity
Hash passwords with a strong password-hashing library.
Use parameterized SQL queries or a safe ORM/data-access layer.
Validate all numeric quantities server-side.
Apply role-based access control to admin/verifier endpoints.
Use secure session handling and CSRF protection for form-based requests.
Use database transactions for wallet and redemption operations.
Prevent duplicate QR/event submissions.
Maintain audit logs for privileged actions.

18. Example End-to-End Scenario
A student joins a campus cycling challenge. They scan the event QR code and submit 8 km of cycling. The backend verifies that the QR token is valid and not previously used. Python reads the active cycling factor from MySQL, calculates EcoPoints and estimated CO₂e savings, stores the activity and impact records, creates a wallet credit transaction, updates the user's challenge progress and streak, checks badge rules, and creates a notification. The dashboard then displays the updated score and progress.

25. Project Differentiator
The project should be presented not as a simple reward application, but as a "verified sustainability intelligence platform." Its differentiator is the combination of a transactional Eco Wallet, configurable impact factors, verified activities, personalized challenge generation, environmental analytics, community missions and anomaly detection in one relational system.
